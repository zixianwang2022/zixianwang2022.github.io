I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC)**, advised by Prof. Minjia Zhang. My research focuses on **high-performance, large-scale LLM training**: scaling Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models on supercomputers, ML systems, and GPU performance optimization.

Before UIUC, I received my B.S. in Computer Science from **UC San Diego**, where I worked with Prof. Leon Bergen, Prof. Ramamohan Paturi, and Prof. Taylor Berg-Kirkpatrick. I also co-led the UCSD team at the SC23 and SC24 Student Cluster Competitions and interned twice at **AMD**.

**Research interests:** Large-scale LLM training & inference systems · Mixture-of-Experts at scale · GPU kernel optimization · High-performance computing
